Privacy Policy

Effective: 16 September 2026

Overview

KPS: Seed Master is designed to work without an account. We do not operate advertising, analytics, or cloud-storage services for the app.

Information stored on your device

The app may store player profiles, display names, preferences, match history, and game statistics locally on your device. This information is used to provide app features and is not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

Local Multiplayer

When you use Local Multiplayer, participating devices communicate directly over the same Wi-Fi network or mobile hotspot. Player names, room information, and game state may be transmitted between those devices. This traffic stays on the local network, although its security also depends on the network you use.

Camera access

Camera access is optional and is used only to scan a Local Multiplayer room QR code. The app does not intentionally save or upload camera images. You can deny camera access and enter room details manually.

Internet and third-party services

The app may use network access for local multiplayer functionality and for resources required by the app platform or included software components. We do not sell personal information.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated account or server. Parents and guardians should supervise device and network use as appropriate.

Data control

You can remove locally stored app data by using available in-app controls, clearing the app’s storage through device settings, or uninstalling the app. Uninstalling may permanently remove local profiles, settings, and statistics.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when the app or its practices change. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer support contact shown on the KPS: Seed Master Google Play listing.
